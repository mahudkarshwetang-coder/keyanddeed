The Open Book

Most players in Canadian real estate hide the one number everyone actually needs: what homes sold for. Boards fought to the Supreme Court to keep sold prices private. The biggest listing site still won't show them. We built the opposite — and wrote the rules down.

Our commitments

  • Never hide a sold price. Every property we touch gets its full public history: list price → every cut → relist attempts → final sale price, with true days-on-market.
  • No days-on-market games. A relist can't reset the clock — our timeline spans MLS numbers.
  • No paywalls, no contact walls. Where regulators require a registered portal, it's a free, instant account. Never "call us for the price."
  • No fake urgency. "Multiple offers" only when true. No teaser pricing, no cherry-picked comps, no steering — our tools rank by fit and math, nothing else.
  • Our own books open too: fees, unit economics, and completion rates published. The standard we demand of the industry, applied to us first.
  • The Open Ledger: every deal closed through the platform flows back into the public comp database — prices and timelines, parties anonymized. Your deals build the commons' data, free to members forever.

What you'll never see from us

"Price withheld." · "Login to view." · Quietly delisted after close. · Days-on-market resets. · "Call us for the price." · Manufactured bidding wars. · Commission hidden in fine print. · Your data sold to lead brokers.

Where our data comes from

Public and government sources — land registry records, municipal open data, CMHC and Statistics Canada — plus member-contributed comps and, through our licensed brokerage partners, the same registered channels every law-abiding brokerage uses. We never scrape MLS. We never hide. That's the whole doctrine.

Rules like these are only worth anything if you can check them. Every number on this site cites its source — start with the Fee Card and How We Make Money. And when we measured the rest of the industry, we published that too: The Receipts — every site's load, side by side, ours included.

Key & Deed — success fees only. We earn when you win. Nothing hidden, ever.
