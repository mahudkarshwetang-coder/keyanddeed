What the ledger says

  • The average measurable site contacts 9 outside companies the moment you land on it.
  • One site (Zown) runs 21 marketing platforms at once — including every major ad network's pixel and software that records your scrolling sessions.
  • One renter site remembers which listings you disliked, in your own browser.
  • Six of seventeen sites refused an automated visitor entirely — you get a bot-block page instead of a website, and we get no numbers to show you.
  • Our row: 1 script. 0 outside companies. 0 saved in your browser.

How we measured — so you can check us

Each site was loaded once in a fresh browser profile (no cookies, no account, no history) on October 15, 2026, and the page's own network activity was captured and kept. Counts are distinct outside domains the page requested from — the site's own domains are excluded. We re-run this monthly and publish the ledger.
