Accessibility

Money decisions are hard enough without the screen fighting back. We're building this site to be usable by everyone — and we'll fix what we get wrong, fast.

Our commitment

  • We target WCAG 2.1 Level AA across the site — the standard referenced by Ontario's AODA and good practice everywhere.
  • Keyboard-first: every tool and link is fully operable without a mouse, with visible focus.
  • Screen-reader friendly: semantic headings, labelled inputs, and results that announce themselves when they change.
  • Readable by default: strong contrast, generous type sizes, no text baked into images, no motion that can't be ignored.
  • Plain language: we write like people talk. If a sentence needs a glossary, we rewrite the sentence instead.

Known limitations

This is v1 — built small, tested on real devices, but not yet audited by an independent accessibility professional. That audit is on our roadmap before public launch, and we'll publish what it finds.
