The Fee Card

$0 until closing. Flat. No percent. Ever. If your deal doesn't close, you pay nothing — not a listing fee, not an hourly, not a retainer. We only get paid when you win.

Paid at closing, from proceeds. MLS, compliance, and paperwork coordination included. Add-ons (pro photos, open house, negotiation lead) priced individually, upfront.

Buy — $500–999 flat

Paid at closing. And we rebate the rest of the buyer-side commission straight back to you. The bigger your deal, the more you keep.

Rent (landlord) — $199–599 flat

Per successful placement: multi-site listing, screening, provincial lease forms, rent analytics. Tenants: always free. We never charge renters.

Rent-to-own — flat at each milestone

Success fees at option signing and at buyout — per the RTO Charter. Nothing upfront, ever.

Commercial — flat at closing

Small commercial leasing and sales, where commissions normally run 2–5%+. Same rule: nothing until you close.

Third-party pros

Lawyers, inspectors, appraisers keep their own fixed fees — shown upfront, paid to them directly, never percentages.
