The fine print that matters ⚖️

  • You can't collect the commission yourself. In Ontario (TRESA), brokerage remuneration is paid under the seller's listing agreement — a self-represented buyer cannot receive it. What you can do is negotiate the saving into the price or a seller credit.
  • It's a negotiation, not a right. Some sellers (and some listing brokers) won't play — outcomes vary by listing agreement, market heat, and how you frame it. That's why the slider exists.
  • Fallback that always works: hire flat-fee licensed representation and take the co-op back as a rebate — contractual, no seller cooperation needed where a co-op was offered.
