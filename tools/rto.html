The formulas (so you can audit us)

  • Buyout = price × (1 + escalation)^years · Wait-path = price × (1 + market growth)^years
  • Credits = deposit + monthly × 12 × years (simple sum, no interest)
  • Net cost basis (if it works) = buyout − credits · Money lost (if it doesn't) = credits × your selected forfeiture %
  • Escalation benchmark: ~5%/yr locked buyback (common in Canadian RTO programs, Fall 2026). Charter mode = capped forfeiture with exit ramps.
  • Estimates only — not advice; every contract differs. Get independent legal advice; the Charter requires it anyway.
