How the score works (v1)

  • Days on market: +25 (>60) · +15 (30–60) · +8 (14–30) · 0 (<14)
  • Price cuts: +8 each, capped at +24 · Competition & market temperature: up to +35 combined
  • Your prep: financing +10 · flexible closing +6 · condition-light +6
  • Opening offer starts at 0–2% under ask (score <25) up to 7–12% under (score 65+); "walk-way ceiling" = full ask, because a number you overpay is worse than a deal you lose.
  • Estimates only — not advice. Real leverage depends on the seller's life story, which no calculator can see. This gets you in the room swinging.
