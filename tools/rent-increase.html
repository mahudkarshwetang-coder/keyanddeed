Above-guideline increases need an LTB order (your landlord applies — Form L5), or in limited cases your written agreement (Form N10, with a 5-day cooling-off). Disputes go to the LTB (Form T1) within 12 months of first paying an improper amount. Care homes, co-ops and social housing have their own rules.

Sources & assumptions

  • Guidelines, 90-day written notice on the proper form, the 12-month rule, the 2.5% cap, exemptions: ontario.ca — Residential rent increases (updated Jun 23, 2026; 2027 = 1.9%, 2026 = 2.1%).
  • "Lowest in four years" context (2.1% for 2026, 2025 = 2.5%): Ontario news release (Jun 30, 2025).
  • Forms + fees: LTB — Forms, filing and fees — N1/N2 notice forms, T1 ($53 / $48 via the portal), above-guideline application L5 ($233 + $10 per additional unit, max $1,000). LTB process updates Sept 21, 2026 touched termination/application forms, not N1/N2.
  • The underlying statute: Residential Tenancies Act, 2006.
  • Verified October 2026. Estimates, not legal advice — your lease and the LTB's rules are the final word. For a live dispute, consider duty counsel or a licensed paralegal.
