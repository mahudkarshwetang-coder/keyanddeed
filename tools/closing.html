Sources & assumptions

  • Ontario LTT bands + 2.5% on the excess over $2M (1–2 unit residential): ontario.ca — Calculating Land Transfer Tax (updated Apr 2026).
  • First-time buyer refund up to $4,000 (never owned anywhere; proportionate for mixed co-buyers): ontario.ca — LTT refunds for first-time homebuyers.
  • Toronto MLTT bands + high-value tiers (4.40%–8.60%, from Apr 1 2026), $102.56+HST admin fee, $4,475 FTHB rebate: toronto.ca — MLTT Rates & Fees (updated Apr 7, 2026).
  • Legal fees, title insurance and disbursement lines: published Ontario firm examples — Barbarian Law ($1,650 + HST purchase, incl. one mortgage) and Kelly Santini LLP (disbursement and title-insurance schedule).
  • Rent equivalence: CMHC Fall 2025 average turnover 2-bedroom rent, Toronto (~$2,540/mo).
  • Bands and fees verified October 2026. Estimates only — not an appraisal, not advice, not an offer of brokerage services. Your lawyer's statement of adjustments is the final word.
