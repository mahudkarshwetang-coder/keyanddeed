Sources & assumptions

  • Baselines: CMHC Rental Market Report, Fall 2025 (October 2025 survey) — purpose-built and condominium apartment averages, 2-bedroom. Turnover = rents paid by new tenants.
  • Bedroom factors (v1 approximations): studio 0.62× · 1-bed 0.80× · 3-bed+ 1.25× of the 2-bedroom average. Feature adds (parking/laundry/reno/furnished) are market heuristics, not CMHC figures. Range = ±6%.
  • Estimates only — not an appraisal, not advice. Every building differs; treat this as your opening number, not gospel.
