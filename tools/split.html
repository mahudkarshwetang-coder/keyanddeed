Sources & assumptions

  • Split models and the royalty convention (70/30 + ~6–8% of GCI off the top; low-split shops at $99–$299/mo + $295–$695/deal): Summitly — low-commission split brokerage comparison, Ontario 2026.
  • Desk fees, splits, caps and fee menus across Canada: BREMO — brokerage fees, desk fees, splits & caps.
  • 100%-commission shop terms ($109/mo + $395–$599 per transaction, published): Right at Home Realty — agent economics. Annual caps vary (Keller Williams ≈ $23k; eXp ≈ C$16k; Real 85/15 + $12k cap) — this tool shows a single deal.
  • Commission context: the FCAC gives a broad 2–6% range, explicitly not an average; fees are set by agreement and competition: PropertyMesh — what you actually pay. TRESA confirms fees are negotiable.
  • Industry-reported models, rounded. Verified October 2026. Estimates only — not an appraisal, not advice; your representation agreement is the only binding number.
